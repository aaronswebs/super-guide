# Example AI/ML Governance Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes governance principles and guidelines for the responsible development, deployment, and use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning systems.

## Core Principles

### 1. Ethical AI Development
- Design AI systems that are fair, transparent, and accountable
- Avoid bias in training data and model outputs
- Conduct regular bias audits and fairness assessments
- Document model limitations and potential failure modes

### 2. Transparency and Explainability
- Maintain clear documentation of AI system capabilities and limitations
- Provide explanations for AI-driven decisions when required
- Disclose when users are interacting with AI systems
- Make model cards available for significant AI deployments

### 3. Data Governance for AI
- Use only authorized and properly licensed data for training
- Ensure data quality, accuracy, and representativeness
- Protect personal data and respect privacy rights
- Implement data lineage tracking for AI training datasets

### 4. Model Development and Testing
- Follow established AI development lifecycle processes
- Conduct thorough testing including edge cases and adversarial testing
- Implement version control for models and training data
- Perform security assessments for AI systems

### 5. Human Oversight
- Maintain human-in-the-loop for critical decisions
- Provide mechanisms for users to challenge AI decisions
- Establish clear escalation paths for AI-related issues
- Regular review of automated decision-making processes

### 6. Risk Management
- Conduct AI impact assessments for new systems
- Identify and mitigate potential risks and harms
- Monitor AI systems continuously for drift and degradation
- Implement fail-safe mechanisms and rollback procedures

### 7. Responsible Use
- Use AI systems only for their intended purposes
- Prohibit use of AI for harmful or discriminatory purposes
- Respect intellectual property and licensing requirements
- Consider societal impact of AI deployments

### 8. Continuous Monitoring
- Implement ongoing monitoring for model performance
- Track and address model drift and data drift
- Regular audits of AI system outputs and impacts
- Incident reporting and response procedures

## Compliance
This policy aligns with EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and ISO/IEC standards for AI.

## Governance Structure
- AI Ethics Board reviews high-risk AI systems
- Data Science teams responsible for technical compliance
- Legal and compliance teams ensure regulatory adherence

## Review
This policy is reviewed bi-annually and updated to reflect evolving best practices.
